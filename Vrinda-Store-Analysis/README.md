# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project involved analyzing the annual sales data of **Vrinda Store** to uncover key trends, top-performing categories, and customer demographics. The goal was to provide data-driven recommendations to help the store improve its sales strategy for the upcoming year.

## Data Analysis Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation
* Standardized order status (Delivered, Cancelled, Refunded).
* Created **Age Group** buckets (Teen, Adult, Senior) to better categorize the customer base.
* Extracted "Month" from order dates to analyze seasonal sales trends.

### 2. Analytical Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
I focused on several critical business metrics:
* **Sales vs Orders:** Comparing total revenue against the number of orders per month.
* **Customer Gender:** Identifying which gender contributes more to total sales.
* **Order Status:** Tracking the percentage of successful deliveries vs. returns.
* **Top 5 States:** Visualizing which regions in India (like Maharashtra or Karnataka) drive the most volume.

### 3. Dashboard Features
* **Dynamic Slicers:** Added filters for **Category, Channel (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra), and Month** for deep-dive analysis.
* **Visual Hierarchy:** Used a clean, Dark Mode layout to highlight the most important sales figures first.

## Key Business Insights
* **Top Channel:** A significant portion of sales comes from **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra**, suggesting a strong e-commerce presence.
* **Gender Split:** Women are the primary customers for Vrinda Store, contributing to ~64% of total sales.
* **Seasonal Peak:** There is a noticeable spike in sales during specific months, likely due to festive seasons.
